Seller Options

Different sellers need different levels of help. Liquor License Market can support a direct listing, a broker-led transaction, or a sale coordinated with legal and closing professionals.

Self-Directed Sale

Best for sellers who want to control pricing, inquiries, and negotiations directly.

  • Create the listing
  • Receive buyer interest
  • Negotiate directly
  • Choose your own closing professionals

Broker-Assisted Sale

Best for sellers who want a licensed intermediary to market the license and manage buyer discussions.

  • Broker representation
  • Pricing and market guidance
  • Buyer qualification
  • Negotiation support

Professional Closing Support

For sellers who already have a buyer but need help coordinating transfer, escrow, legal, or financing matters.

  • Attorney coordination
  • Escrow support
  • Transfer documentation
  • Lender coordination when needed
