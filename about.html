About LLM

A National Marketplace Built Around State Markets

Liquor License Market provides a national starting point with permanent state-market pages beneath it, creating a clear structure for visitors and search engines.

National at the Top, Local Underneath

The national site organizes markets by state rather than treating every page as part of one undifferentiated directory. Each state page can become the parent for local markets, license categories, inventory and state-specific information.

Florida remains dedicated

Florida is connected through its own state hub and dedicated Florida Liquor License Market website while the national LLM site remains the parent gateway.
