Become an LLM Lender

Tell LLM what types of business-purpose liquor-license transactions you are prepared to review. The goal is to match lender criteria with relevant borrower requests without treating every opportunity as suitable for every lender.

Define your lending box

This page currently defines the information LLM should collect from participating lenders. A connected submission workflow can be added so these preferences are stored and used for matching.

What participation means

Control

You decide what to review

Set geography, loan-size, collateral and transaction preferences so irrelevant requests can be filtered out.

Independence

You underwrite the transaction

LLM can organize information, but each lender independently evaluates credit, collateral, documentation and compliance.

No Obligation

Review does not require funding

Receiving or reviewing a transaction does not obligate a lender to quote terms, approve a loan or fund a transaction.

Before making a loan

  • Confirm that your entity and proposed transaction comply with applicable lending and licensing laws.
  • Verify the borrower's identity, authority, financial information and intended business purpose.
  • Confirm license status, transferability and any existing liens or claims.
  • Use appropriate legal documentation for the loan, collateral and closing.
  • Make your own determination of collateral value and repayment risk.
LLM's role: facilitate discovery and information flow between borrowers and lenders. LLM does not guarantee loans, returns, collateral recovery or the accuracy of information supplied by transaction participants.
