Quick answers

Not sure which license you need?

Start with the business activity—restaurant, bar, package store, beer and wine, full liquor—then work backward to the correct state license class.

Not sure what a license is worth?

Look at scarcity, geography, license rights, status, current competing inventory and recent market activity rather than relying on a single asking price.
