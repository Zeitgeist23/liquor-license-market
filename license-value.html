What Is My Liquor License Worth?

Liquor license values can vary sharply by state, county or municipality, license class, transferability, market supply, and current buyer demand. We help sellers frame an asking price using the market that actually applies to their license.

License Type

Full-liquor, quota, package-store, beer-and-wine, and special-use licenses can trade in very different price ranges.

Location

County, municipality, population limits, local issuance rules, and buyer demand often have the largest effect on value.

Transfer Status

Transfer eligibility, liens, escrow status, operating history, and regulatory issues can affect both price and closing speed.

Current Market

Recent asking prices, competing listings, and active buyer interest help establish a realistic market range.
