Professional Marketplace Access

For Brokers & Professionals

Liquor License Market is designed to work alongside the professionals who help buyers and sellers complete license transactions, not around them.

Brokers

Market client inventory, expose listings to additional buyers, and direct interested parties to the listing professional handling the transaction.

Attorneys

Help clients structure purchase agreements, address transfer contingencies, resolve title or ownership issues, and coordinate transaction documents.

Escrow & Closing

Coordinate deposits, closing conditions, payoff requirements, and release of funds as regulatory and contractual conditions are satisfied.

Lenders & Investors

Connect financing sources with qualified buyers and license owners seeking acquisition or refinance capital where permitted.
