What LLM is designed to do

Organize the transaction

Put license, borrower, amount, geography and collateral information into a consistent format.

Match relevant participants

Direct borrower requests toward lenders whose stated criteria appear to fit the transaction.

Let lenders underwrite independently

Each lender determines whether to request additional information, offer terms or decline.

LLM is a marketplace, not a promise of financing. Loan availability, rates, terms, collateral requirements and closing are determined by participating lenders and subject to applicable law.
