Financing / Request Review

Get Pre-Qualified

A pre-qualification request gives potential lenders the core information they need to decide whether a liquor-license transaction may fit their lending criteria. It is not a commitment to lend or a guarantee of approval.

Start with the transaction basics

This page currently organizes the information typically needed for review. Submitting an actual financing request will require a connected intake workflow; no information entered here is transmitted unless that workflow is added.

Documents that can strengthen a request

Transaction

Purchase or payoff documents

Purchase agreement, letter of intent, seller note, payoff statement or other documents showing the transaction structure.

Business

Financial support

Tax returns, profit-and-loss statements, bank statements, projections or acquisition financials can help a lender assess repayment capacity.

License

License information

License number, class, status, jurisdiction, transfer restrictions and evidence of ownership or purchase rights.

What pre-qualification means

Pre-qualification is preliminary. It may indicate that a transaction appears potentially financeable based on initial information. Final approval can require full underwriting, verification, valuation, legal documentation and regulatory review.
