From Listing to Closing

How Selling Works

The exact transfer process varies by state and locality, but most liquor-license sales follow the same basic path.

1

Prepare the License

Confirm the license type, jurisdiction, status, transferability, ownership information, and any liens or escrow issues that may affect a sale.

2

Set the Asking Price

Compare the license with available inventory, market demand, and other relevant transactions in the same licensing market.

3

List and Receive Inquiries

Present the license to qualified buyers and provide enough information for them to evaluate the opportunity without unnecessarily exposing confidential seller information.

4

Negotiate and Document the Sale

Agree on price, deposits, contingencies, closing timing, transfer responsibilities, and any financing or escrow terms.

5

Transfer and Close

Complete the required regulatory filings, escrow or closing process, and final payment once the agreed conditions have been satisfied.
